Pipeline Overview

StepSub-packageAPI
1–4SmoreBaseSMFitProblem, fitSurrogate
5SmoreBasequantifyUncertainty (profile likelihood)
6SmoreBasesampleSMPredictions
7SmoreGSArunSensitivity
8SmoreFitbuildPosterior, posteriorScore, inPosterior

Steps 1–6 fit a surrogate model (SM) to complex model (CM) output and quantify its parameter uncertainty. The shared backbone from there is a per-CM-parameter-set ProfileLikelihoodResult:

  • Step 7 (SmoreGSA) treats those profiles as an uncertainty envelope and asks how SM output varies as CM parameters change.
  • Step 8 (SmoreFit) treats those profiles as one side of a comparison and asks which CM parameter values are consistent with real-world data.

Same upstream cost, two complementary downstream answers. See SmoreExamples.jl for a full walkthrough — logistic_growth_pipeline.jl for Steps 1–7, cm_posterior_pipeline.jl for Step 8.
